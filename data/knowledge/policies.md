# Meeting and attendance rules (mock policy for the demo)

## Attendance
Students must keep at least 75% attendance in every course. A student with CGPA 9.0 or above is exempt from the minimum attendance rule. The agent only suggests a meeting over a class when skipping that class keeps attendance at least 2% above the minimum, or the student is exempt.

## Meeting hours
Faculty-student meetings are scheduled between 08:00 and 19:00, Monday to Saturday. No meetings are scheduled on institute holidays. Students are kept free for one hour before every exam.

## Cabin hours
Each faculty member publishes cabin hours. The agent prefers cabin hours when it suggests times to meet a faculty member. Meetings with faculty happen at the faculty cabin unless stated otherwise.

## Exams
CAT-2 and FAT exams follow the academic calendar. Regular classes do not run on exam days. The agent avoids booking meetings on a student's exam day when possible.

## Privacy
The agent shares only free or busy status with other people. It never tells anyone why a person is busy. Uploaded personal commitments are visible only to the person who uploaded them.

## Reminders
Everyone in a booked meeting gets a reminder one day before and thirty minutes before the meeting, and the meeting is added to their calendar.

## Rescheduling
If a faculty member becomes unavailable, every affected meeting is moved to the nearest time that works for all participants, and everyone is notified.
